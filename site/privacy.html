Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Drive Backup is a personal desktop app that copies files from folders you choose on your computer to your own Google Drive.

What data the app accesses

  • Your chosen files: only files inside folders you select in the app.
  • Google Drive: the app uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. It can only create and update files it created itself. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Your Google account email address and storage usage: shown in the app's settings window so you can see which account is signed in and how much space is used.

Where your data goes

Your files are sent directly from your computer to your own Google Drive using Google's official API. They are not sent to the developer or to any other server or third party. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising.

What is stored on your computer

The app keeps, on your own computer only: your list of chosen folders, a record of which files have been uploaded, a log file, and your Google sign-in token (readable only by your user account).

Deleting your data

You can stop the app and remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uploaded files stay in the Drive Backup folder in your Google Drive until you delete them yourself.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Drive Backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
